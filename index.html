1. Audio and backups

JokeTap stores your recordings, imported sounds, board and button names, playback settings and sound credits in app storage on your device. These are used to create, organise and play your soundboards. You do not need a JokeTap account.

Microphone permission is used for recording when you choose that feature. You can revoke it in Android settings. Importing and exporting use Android’s file picker to access the file or destination you select.

Exported backups contain your library, including original audio files and audio outside the selected playback trim. Backups are not encrypted by JokeTap. Keep them somewhere you trust. If you choose a cloud storage provider, that provider receives the exported file under its own privacy terms. Deleting a sound in JokeTap does not delete existing backups or the original file you imported.

The app is configured to exclude its private data from Android automatic cloud backup and device transfer. Use JokeTap’s explicit backup feature when you want to move your library.

Temporary evaluation workspace

Evaluation access uses a separate temporary workspace with sample sounds, opened with an evaluation code. The code is checked on your device, not sent to a JokeTap server. Evaluation does not create a purchase or change your ordinary library, theme or purchase status. Its working library is discarded when evaluation ends. After an interrupted process, temporary audio files may remain in Android's app cache until cleanup or cache removal. Backups you explicitly export remain at your chosen destination and do not grant Plus access.

2. Google Play purchases and diagnostics

Google Play processes purchases of JokeTap Plus. The app receives purchase status and signed receipt information, including product and transaction identifiers and a purchase token, to check, acknowledge and restore access. It stores an encrypted receipt cache on the device for offline access; this is excluded from soundboard backups.

We can view order information made available to developers by Google Play to handle purchases and refunds. JokeTap does not receive your full payment-card details. Google maintains its own payment records under its terms and privacy policy.

Google Play also handles redemption of Plus promo codes. You enter the promo code in Google Play, not in JokeTap. JokeTap checks the resulting entitlement through the same purchase and restoration mechanisms. Promo codes are different from temporary evaluation codes.

The Google Play Billing library also automatically submits technical diagnostic events and app/device metadata to Google, including billing errors, app version, Android/device build information, network type and locale settings. This diagnostic monitoring is separate from your sound library; JokeTap does not attach recordings or board names to it. We do not operate our own analytics server or receive a copy of these SDK events. Google's handling and retention follow its applicable notices; clearing JokeTap's storage does not delete Google's records.

3. Advertising tests and privacy choices

The current Google Play release (version code 7) does not include network advertising or the AdMob/UMP SDKs. Free access can display an on-device promotion for JokeTap Plus. A separate development build tests Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP); the following advertising disclosures apply only when those development tests are enabled.

The development build can store an age range locally to decide whether an advertising test is eligible. It does not ask for a date of birth. The range is excluded from soundboard backups and can be changed in Settings → Privacy choices. The initial test route is for users selecting 18 or over; it does not request ads for younger or unanswered age ranges.

When enabled, Google’s advertising and consent services process device and network information, consent choices and interactions for consent management, ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Google documents IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), app/ad interactions, diagnostics and device or account identifiers as SDK data categories. The development configuration removes Android advertising-ID permission and requests non-personalised test ads. This does not mean that no data is processed. See Google’s SDK data disclosure, how Google uses information from partner apps and Google’s privacy policy.

JokeTap does not supply your audio, board names or purchase receipts as advertising-request content. Where a consent choice is required, you can accept, refuse or manage it. Available privacy options are accessible through Settings → Privacy choices. Refusing advertising consent does not prevent local soundboard use. We will update this policy before introducing advertising in a Play release.

4. Support and service providers

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, investigate problems and handle privacy requests. Send only information needed for your enquiry; do not send passwords or payment-card details. Support email is handled through Gmail.

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit it there, GitHub processes website connection information under its privacy statement. The page itself contains no analytics scripts, advertising, external fonts or tracking cookies.

Google processes information for its Play, Gmail and advertising services under the applicable service terms and privacy notices; it is not merely our storage agent for every purpose. GitHub determines processing for its hosting-service operations. A file provider you select handles your exports under its own terms. We do not send your sound library to these services except through an export you choose or an attachment you send yourself.

These providers may process data outside Poland and the EEA, including in the United States. Google describes adequacy decisions, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses among its transfer safeguards; GitHub describes its safeguards in its privacy statement. Which safeguard applies depends on the service, recipient and transfer. Contact us for information about safeguards applicable to processing for which we are responsible. We do not claim that every transfer uses the same mechanism.

5. Retention, deletion and your rights

Your local library remains until you delete content, replace the library through restoration, clear the app’s storage or uninstall it. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes local app data, including saved preferences and the cached receipt; Google Play may restore a valid purchase when you use the app again. Remove exported backups separately from their storage destinations.

For information we receive outside your device, we use the following retention schedule:

  • Routine support messages: delete within 12 months after the enquiry is closed, to allow relevant follow-up and recurring-problem investigation.
  • Audio and diagnostic attachments: delete within 30 days after closure unless a specific unresolved complaint or dispute requires them. Keep only the necessary evidence in that case.
  • Purchase, refund, complaint and legal-claim records: keep the minimum records needed until the relevant obligation, proceeding or applicable limitation period ends. Review continued need at least annually; these records are not all ordinary support email.
  • Accounting and tax records: keep only the records required by applicable Polish accounting or tax law for the period that law requires, taking account of legally applicable extensions. The business’s accounting retention schedule controls those records, not the 12-month support rule.

We apply deletion to our working copies, including downloaded support attachments. Providers may retain their own backups, security logs and legally required records for periods described in their policies. Google’s purchase and consent records and your chosen storage provider’s files follow those providers’ rules. We cannot remotely delete files held only on your phone.

Where GDPR applies, our purposes and legal bases are:

  • Providing requested functionality and fulfilling Plus purchases, restoration and contractual support: performance of the contract or steps requested before it (Article 6(1)(b)). Local sound processing does not give us remote access to your recordings.
  • General enquiries, troubleshooting, service security and establishing or defending legal claims: legitimate interests in responding to you, maintaining a functioning service and protecting legal rights (Article 6(1)(f)), balanced against your interests and rights.
  • Required accounting, tax and consumer-law records and responses: compliance with the applicable legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)).
  • Optional advertising-related processing requiring consent: your consent (Article 6(1)(a)), together with any consent required for access to or storage on your device. Consent choices do not authorise unrelated uses. Google explains its own purposes and bases in its notices.

Providing information for a general enquiry is voluntary, but we need a return address and enough detail to answer. Purchase information is needed to supply and verify Plus; information legally required for records is mandatory where the relevant law applies. Declining optional advertising consent does not disable local audio features. A microphone permission prompt is an Android access control, not a blanket agreement to unrelated processing.

You may have rights to access, correct, erase or obtain a portable copy of your data, restrict processing and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can withdraw consent through available privacy controls without affecting earlier lawful processing. Email apperatork@gmail.com; we normally respond to GDPR requests within one month and will explain any lawful extension. We may ask for proportionate information to verify a request, but will not ask for your password.

You may complain to your local supervisory authority, including Poland’s President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO). You do not have to contact us first. We do not operate a JokeTap account system or make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects through automated profiling.

6. Younger users

JokeTap is intended for people aged 13 and over. Do not send us personal information about a child under 13. If you believe such information was sent to our support address, contact us so we can investigate and remove it where appropriate. This audience description does not replace any additional protections required for teenagers in their region.

7. Security and changes

JokeTap uses Android’s app-private storage and encrypts its cached purchase receipt using a key held in Android Keystore. Your device’s security and your choice of backup destination also matter; exported backups are not protected by that receipt encryption. Google states that its advertising SDK encrypts data in transit using TLS.

We will revise this page when relevant functionality or data practices change and update its date. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate in-app notice or release information.
